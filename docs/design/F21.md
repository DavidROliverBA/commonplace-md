# F21 — A timeline on the graph (done 2026-10-25, v0.0.50)

The design as planned, kept for reference. What was built differs in small ways, noted at the end.

## The plan

**Size** M · **Evidence** `src/graph.ts` sends no dates; imported and GitHub vaults show every
note as made on the import day (`notes.created_at`).

1. **Each note's date**, in order: a daily note's date; a date property (`date:`, `created:`, or
   the first date-kind property of its type, or the vault type's rule from F20); else when it
   was made. `graph()` sends `when` and which rule gave it. Optionally, importing a zip keeps
   each file's date as the note's made date.
2. **Timeline** in the graph's bar opens a strip along the bottom: notes per month as small
   bars, and a range with two handles. Notes outside fade (or hide, a choice); a link shows only
   when both its notes are in range. A Programme's start and end dates bound the range, and its
   milestones are marked on the strip.
3. **Play** grows the graph from the first month to the last in about ten seconds; touching the
   graph pauses it. Positions are worked out once for every note, so nothing jumps. With Reduce
   Motion it steps a month at a time.
4. Handles are sliders (arrows a week, Page Up and Down a month); a live region says "Showing 42
   notes, March to June 2025". Works with every scope and filter; the range goes in the address.
   On the phone the strip is one row.
5. Not yet: when each link was made (links have no dates; an additive column could come later).

**Done when** the range and Play work on the laptop and the iPhone in both themes, with unit
tests for the date rules, a browser test, and axe on the strip.


## As built

- Arrow keys move a handle a **day** (the slider's step), not a week; Page Up and Page Down a month.
- A type names its timeline property with `timeline: <property>` in its own note; otherwise its
  first date property counts, except birthday-like ones (`birthday`, `born`, `died`,
  `anniversary`, `dob`), so a Person isn't placed in the year they were born.
- A month's bar can be clicked to see just that month; **All dates** goes back.
- On the phone the bars give way to the handles, and the find button steps aside.
- Not built: a Programme's start and end dates and milestones on the strip (they need F20), and
  keeping a zip's file dates on import (left for when imports are next touched).
