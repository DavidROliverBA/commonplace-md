# F17 — One Settings screen: every setting lives there, as a principle

_The design as agreed before it was built. Done: see [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md); what's still open is in [BACKLOG.md](../BACKLOG.md)._

**Built 2026-10-12 (v0.0.22).**

**Size** S–M · **Evidence** settings are split across two modals opened from different places:
**Account** (`web/components/Account.tsx`: profile, password, AI assistants, Share from iPhone,
Connect Claude, GitHub, admin), from the account button at the foot of the sidebar; and **Vault
settings** (`web/components/VaultSettings.tsx`: name, members, import, export, leave or delete),
from the sidebar's gear. Commands `vault.settings` and `vault.account` in `web/lib/commands.ts`.
Each new feature has so far added its section wherever seemed closest.

**The principle:** anything you set once and that stays set (preferences, connections, keys and
tokens, members, vault options) is found in **Settings**, and nowhere else. Controls that change
the view you're looking at (sort order, edit or read mode, panel toggles, task filters) stay
where they act; they're remembered, but they aren't settings. A feature can offer a shortcut to
its setting where it's needed (the AI panel's "add an assistant", the GitHub vault dialog's
connect step), but the shortcut opens that section of Settings, not a second copy of it.

**Done when:**
- `docs/PRINCIPLES.md` states the rule, and its checklist gains "Any new setting goes in
  Settings; nothing to configure anywhere else".
- One Settings screen with sections: Account (profile, password), This vault (name, members,
  import, export, leave or delete), AI assistants, Connections (Claude, GitHub, Share from
  iPhone), Admin (admins only). On the iPhone it's a list of sections, each opening in the sheet.
- The gear and the account button both open it, at the right section. There's one command per
  section, so ⌘K "github" or "members" lands on it.
- In-context shortcuts open the Settings section instead of embedding their own form.
- No setting-like control is left outside Settings (checked by a search of `web/components`).
- `bun run screens` checked on laptop and phone, both themes, and the UI tests pass.
