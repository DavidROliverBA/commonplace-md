# Strawberry jam

- 1 kg [[Strawberries]], hulled
- 1 kg jam sugar (with pectin)
- Juice of a lemon

1. Layer the fruit and sugar in a pan and leave overnight.
2. Heat gently until the sugar dissolves, then boil hard for 4 minutes.
3. Test on a cold saucer: if it wrinkles when pushed, it's ready.
4. Leave for 15 minutes so the fruit doesn't float, then pot.
