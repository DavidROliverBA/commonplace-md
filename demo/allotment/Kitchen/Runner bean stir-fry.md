# Runner bean stir-fry

A quick supper for the days when the [[Runner beans]] win.

- 400 g runner beans, sliced thinly on the diagonal
- 2 cloves [[Garlic]], sliced
- A thumb of ginger, cut into matchsticks
- 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil

Fry the garlic and ginger for a minute, add the beans and a splash of water, and cook for 4 minutes on a high heat. Finish with the soy sauce and sesame oil.
