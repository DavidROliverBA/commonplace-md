# Green tomato chutney

Makes six jars. Keeps for a year in a cool cupboard.

## Ingredients

- 1 kg green [[Tomatoes]], chopped
- 500 g cooking apples, peeled and chopped
- 2 onions, chopped
- 300 g brown sugar
- 400 ml cider vinegar
- 1 tsp each of mustard seed and ground ginger

## Method

1. Put everything in a large pan and bring slowly to the boil, stirring until the sugar dissolves.
2. Simmer uncovered for about 2 hours, until a spoon drawn across the bottom leaves a trail.
3. Pot into hot sterilised jars and seal.

> [!tip]
> Leave it for a month before opening; the vinegar softens.
