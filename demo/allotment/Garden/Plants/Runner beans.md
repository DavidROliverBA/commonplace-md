# Runner beans

A wigwam of eight canes gives more beans than two people can eat.

- Sow outside in mid-May, two seeds per cane.
- Water well once flowering starts; dry roots mean flowers drop without setting.
- ==Pick every two days==, or the plants stop making new pods.
