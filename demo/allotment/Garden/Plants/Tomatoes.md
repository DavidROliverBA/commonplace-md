# Tomatoes

Grown as cordons up canes, side shoots pinched out weekly. 'Sungold' for eating straight off the plant, 'Shirley' for cooking.

- Sow indoors in March at 20 °C, plant out after the last frost (late May).
- Feed weekly with comfrey or tomato feed once the first truss sets.
- Stop the plants at five trusses in August so the fruit ripens.

> [!warning] Blight
> Brown patches on leaves in a warm, wet August mean blight. Take the affected leaves off at once; see [[Pests and problems]].

Unripe ones at the end of the season go into [[Green tomato chutney]].
