# Garlic

The easiest crop on the plot: plant in autumn, ignore over winter, lift in July.

1. Split a bulb into cloves and plant them 15 cm apart, pointed end up, just below the surface.
2. Weed in spring; garlic hates competition.
3. Lift when the lower leaves yellow, and dry in the shed for two weeks.
