# Strawberries

Twelve plants of 'Cambridge Favourite' in the raised bed by the gate.

- Tuck straw under the fruit in May to keep it clean.
- Net against birds as soon as the fruit colours.
- Peg down three runners in August to replace the oldest plants.

The best of the crop goes into [[Strawberry jam]].
