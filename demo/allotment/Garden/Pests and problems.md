# Pests and problems

| Problem | Crop | What helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blight | [[Tomatoes]] | Remove affected leaves, water the soil not the leaves |
| Blackfly | [[Runner beans]] | Pinch out the tips, spray with soapy water |
| Birds | [[Strawberries]] | Netting, pegged down all round |
| Rust | [[Garlic]] | Nothing much; the bulbs are still fine to eat |
| Slugs | Everything | Beer traps, and a torch walk after rain |

> [!note] No sprays
> The site's rules allow no chemical sprays, so everything here is by hand.
