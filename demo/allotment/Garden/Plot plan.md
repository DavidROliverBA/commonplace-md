# Plot plan

Plot 14 is 10 m by 5 m, split into four beds with a path down the middle.

![[plot-plan.svg]]

## Crop rotation

Each bed moves on one step every spring, so no family grows in the same soil two years running.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[Bed 1: legumes] --> B[Bed 2: brassicas]
  B --> C[Bed 3: roots and onions]
  C --> D[Bed 4: potatoes and tomatoes]
  D --> A
```

| Bed | 2026 | 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [[Runner beans]], peas | Cabbages, kale |
| 2 | Cabbages, kale | Carrots, [[Garlic]] |
| 3 | Carrots, [[Garlic]] | Potatoes, [[Tomatoes]] |
| 4 | Potatoes, [[Tomatoes]] | [[Runner beans]], peas |

[[Strawberries]] stay put in the raised bed by the gate and are replaced every three years.
