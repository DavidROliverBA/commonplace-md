# The Well-Tempered Garden

## Summary

Opinionated, funny essays on plants and how to grow them. Not a manual, more a conversation with someone who has gardened for fifty years.

## Highlights

- Gardens should be lived in, not kept tidy for their own sake.
- Try plants in the "wrong" place; sometimes they're happier there.
