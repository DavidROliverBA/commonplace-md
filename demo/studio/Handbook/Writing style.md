# Writing style

Write the way you'd explain it across the desk.

- Short sentences. One idea each.
- Say what a thing does, not what it is "designed to" do.
- UK spelling: colour, organise, programme (but "program" for software).
- Dates as 6 October 2026, never 06/10/26.

> [!example] Before and after
> **Before:** The aforementioned functionality is designed to facilitate ticket purchasing.
> **After:** You can buy tickets here.

> [!warning] Avoid
> "Simply", "just" and "easy": they make anyone who finds it hard feel worse.
