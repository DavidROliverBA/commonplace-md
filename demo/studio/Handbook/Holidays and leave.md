# Holidays and leave

- **25 days** a year plus bank holidays, and the studio closes between Christmas and New Year on top.
- Book at least two weeks ahead for anything over three days, on the studio calendar.
- ==Nobody checks messages on holiday.== Hand over before you go.

## Sick leave

Tell the studio lead by 9:30 if you can't work. No note needed for the first week.

## Parental leave

Full pay for the first 16 weeks, for every parent.
