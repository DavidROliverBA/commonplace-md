# Glossary

- **Content audit:** a list of every page on the old site, with what to keep, change or drop.
- **Sitemap:** the pages the new site will have and how they link. Agreed before design.
- **Round:** two weeks of design and build ending in something clickable. See [[How we work]].
- **Studio lead:** signs off estimates and runs Monday planning.
